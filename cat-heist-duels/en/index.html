How to play

Matches for 2 to 4 players. Everyone plays their turn when they can and the app notifies the next player.

🎡

Spin the wheel

You land on a museum room: each one is a different mini-game.

😼

Keep stealing

As long as you succeed, you keep playing and fill the loot bar. Miss and the turn passes.

💎

Master heist

With a full bar you go for a jewel. In groups, you can steal one from another player.

Six rooms, six heists

Short mini-games of reflexes, memory and nerves of steel.
