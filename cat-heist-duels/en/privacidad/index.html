Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Cat Heist Duels app (iOS and Android) processes, why, and what rights you have. If anything is unclear, write to us.

1. Controller

Alec Lagarde, independent developer based in Spain. Privacy contact: privacidad.catheist@aleclagarde.com. General support: catheist@aleclagarde.com.

2. What data we process and why

We do not sell your data or use it for anything other than the above.

3. Who else processes data (processors)

  • Supabase: the app's database and accounts.
  • Expo (650 Industries), Apple and Google (Firebase): sending notifications.
  • RevenueCat and the stores (Apple App Store, Google Play): purchase management. We never receive your payment details.
  • Google AdMob: ads. You can read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. When they do, they use the safeguards required by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

4. Ads and your consent

The first time an ad is about to be shown, the app asks whether you accept personalised ads. On iPhone it also asks for tracking permission. If you decline, you'll see non-personalised ads and the game works the same. You can change your choice at any time in Friends › Ads. If you buy “No ads”, you won't see ads between turns.

5. How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. If you delete it, we delete your profile, friends, items, currencies and matches immediately; backups are overwritten within 30 days at most. Purchase records may be kept for as long as tax law requires.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: Friends › Delete account. You can also request it by email at privacidad.catheist@aleclagarde.com.

7. Children

Cat Heist Duels is not directed at children under 14. If you are under 14, you need permission from your parent or guardian to use the app. If you believe a child has given us data without that permission, write to us and we'll delete it.

8. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to privacidad.catheist@aleclagarde.com. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

9. Security

Communications are encrypted and each player can only read their own data and the data of the matches they take part in.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, if the change is significant, we'll let you know in the app.
